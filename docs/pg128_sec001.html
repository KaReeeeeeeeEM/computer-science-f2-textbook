Activity 3.5:

Representing algorithms for real-world

tasks using flowcharts and pseudocode

Aim: To apply knowledge of flowcharts

and pseudocode to represent instructions

for real-world tasks.

Materials: Paper and pens/pencils

(for drawing flowcharts); computers or tablets with flowchart software

(optional); access to textbooks or

Internet for additional information (for

example, from YouTube)

Tasks:

1. Taking a bath

(a) Instructions: Break down the steps

for taking a bath into clear, easy-

to-follow actions

(b) Flowchart task: Draw a flowchart

to represent the steps for taking

a bath.

(c) Pseudocode task: Write

pseudocode to represent the

instructions for taking a bath.

2. Finding the largest number in a list

(a) Instructions: Create an algorithm to find the largest number in a list

by comparing each number one by

one until the largest is identified.

(b) Flowchart task: Draw a flowchart to show how to compare numbers and determine the largest.

(c) Pseudocode

task:

Write

a

pseudocode that follows the steps to find the largest number in a list.

3. Giving directions

(a) Instructions:

Imagine

giving

someone directions to a place (for

example, walking to the nearest shop) as shown in Figure 3.7.

Break down the steps into simple,

clear instructions (such as turn left,

walk straight).

(b) Flowchart task: Draw a flowchart

showing the directions as a

sequence of steps.

(c) Pseudocode

task:

Write

a

pseudocode that outlines the steps

to follow to reach the destination.
