Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is a wireless technology that allows

you to connect your phone, tablet, or

computer to the Internet without using

any physical cables or wires. Wi-Fi uses

radio signals to transmit data from a

router to your device, making it possible

to access the Internet anywhere within

the signal range. Wi-Fi can be used at

home, school, cafes, libraries, or other

free public spaces. Figure 1.10 shows the

Wi-Fi connection icons.

Figure 1.10: Wi-Fi connection icons

Activity 1.9:

Identifying Wi-Fi networks

Aim: Recognize and list available

Wi-Fi networks using digital devices,

and understand the basic process of

connecting to a network.

Materials:

Laptop,

tablet,

or

smartphone; Wi-Fi-enabled computer

lab or classroom; Access to a public or school Wi-Fi network (with password, if needed).

Instructions: Use digital devices to

identify Wi-Fi network connectivity in your vicinity.

Steps:

Step 1: Open the “Settings” menu on

your device (laptop, tablet, or

smartphone) and identify Wi-Fi

connection icons.

Step 2: Navigate to the Wi-Fi or

Network and Internet option.

Step 3: Tap or click on Wi-Fi to view

a list of available networks

nearby.

Step 4: Look through the list and identify the names (SSIDs) of visible Wi-Fi networks

(such

as,

SchoolWiFi,

HomeNetwork, PublicWiFi).

Step 5: Choose a network you have

access to (such as, your

school’s Wi-Fi). If required,

enter the password (ask your teacher if needed).
