Online auctions

In some cases, e-commerce is practised

by direct selling of goods and services

among customers through bidding in

online auctions. Bidding is a part of an

auction that allows prospective buyers

to offer a price for an item. For example, on eBay, customers are allowed to quote

online the prices for commodities. A person who quotes higher than others is allowed to pay for the commodity.

Then the goods are sent to him/her via

a courier service such as Post Cargo,

EMS, or DHL. In some cases, auctions

take place online, but there is an option to

physically collect the goods, for example,

the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA)

online auction.

Online finance

One major application of e-commerce in

finance is electronic banking (e-banking),

which enables customers to check

account balances, transfer funds, and

pay bills online. These services are

typically accessed through mobile apps

or web platforms provided by financial

institutions. In addition to e-banking,

mobile money services have become

widely used for sending and receiving

money, purchasing goods and services,

and managing everyday financial

transactions. Another example is online

stock trading, where investors buy and sell

shares electronically through platforms

linked to national stock exchanges.

Manufacturing

E-commerce is used in the supply

chain operations of companies. Some

companies form an electronic exchange

by providing both bought and sold goods,

trade market information, and run back-

office information such as inventory

control. This speeds up the flow of raw materials and finished goods.

Online forums

These are online services where

people contribute to verbal and written

discussions about a particular subject.

To participate in the discussion forum, a

person is sometimes required to subscribe

using an email address. Examples of

online forums include Quora and Slide

Share. Online forums can be used for

both academic and social purposes.

Video conferencing

Video conferencing enables real-time communication between people in

different locations. Using Internet-based

platforms, users can hold virtual meetings,

attend online classes, and stay connected

with family or colleagues. These platforms

often offer features such as screen sharing,

file sharing, chat messaging, virtual whiteboards, and session recording to support collaboration and learning.

Example is a teacher hosting a virtual

class for 50 students via Zoom.
