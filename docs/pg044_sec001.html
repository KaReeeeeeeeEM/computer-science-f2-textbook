Cloud computing and storage

Cloud computing is a way to use the

Internet to store, manage, and access data and programs instead of saving

them directly on your computer or phone.

This means your files are kept safely on

remote servers (big computers) owned

by companies, and you can reach them

anytime and anywhere using the Internet.

Three examples of popular cloud storage

services you might have heard of are

Google drive, OneDrive and Dropbox.

These services let you save documents,

photos, videos, and other files online.

This helps you keep your important data safe, free up space on your device, and easily share files with others.

Benefits of cloud storage

(a) Access anywhere: You can open your

files from any device connected to

the Internet.

(b) Data backup: Your files are safe even

if your computer or phone breaks.

(c) Easy sharing: Share files or folders

with others quickly by sending links.

(d) Collaboration: Work on the same

document with friends or colleagues

at the same time.

(e) Save space: Free up storage on your

device by keeping files in the cloud.

Activity 1.20:

Exploring and using cloud storage

platforms

Aim: To introduce students to cloud storage technology and demonstrate

how to create, save, organize, and share

digital files using platforms such as

Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.

Materials: Smartphone, tablet, or

computer with Internet access, A Google

or Microsoft account (set up beforehand

if needed), Access to Google Drive,

Dropbox or OneDrive, Exercise book

or A4 paper for taking notes

Instructions:

1. Search on YouTube:

Type the following phrase in the

YouTube search bar: “How to use Google Drive (or OneDrive and Dropbox.) for beginners”.

Watch at least one clear and simple tutorial (or use a pre- downloaded video if there’s limited Internet).

2. Sign In and Explore:

Log in to either Google Drive or

OneDrive or Dropbox using your account. Explore the dashboard.

3. Practice basic tasks:

(a) Create a new folder and name it “My Learning Folder”
