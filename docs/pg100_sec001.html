Project 2: Recognising cybercrime cases in your society

Conduct mini-research on at least 15 ways mobile phones are used to deceive

individuals into giving their financial details or personal information to unknown

people. Identify the different methods used to manipulate them. Then, suggest

strategies to prevent such fraudulent activities. Write a report on your findings using

word processing software. The presentation of your data should include graphs

organised using a spreadsheet program.
