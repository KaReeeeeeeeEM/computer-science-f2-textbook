Hacking

Hacking is unauthorised access or breach of computer systems, often by exploiting

security vulnerabilities. Hackers may be motivated by malicious intent, curiosity,

or even ethical reasons. Proper security measures, like software updates and strong passwords, are essential to protect against hacking.

There are different types of hackers, often classified based on their intentions, as

presented in Table 2.9.

Table 2.9: Types of hackers

Hacker

Description

Black hat hackers

These are the “bad” hackers who break into systems with malicious intent, such as stealing data or causing harm.

White hat hackers

These are ethical hackers who work to improve security

by identifying and fixing vulnerabilities.
