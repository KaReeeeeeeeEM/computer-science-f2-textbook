Communication between clients and servers follows a request-response pattern.

Clients send requests to servers specifying the desired service or resource. The server

processes these requests, performs the necessary actions, and sends the results back to

the clients. This exchange typically occurs through well-established communication protocols, as shown in Figure 1.18.
