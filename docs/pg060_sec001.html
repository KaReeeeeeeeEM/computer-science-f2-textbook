Cyber threats and attacks

In today’s interconnected digital world,

cyber threats and attacks have become

increasinglysophisticatedandwidespread,

posing serious risks to individuals,

organizations, and governments. A

threat is any event or situation that has

the potential to cause harm to a computer

system or an organization, whether it

occurs accidentally or intentionally.

It represents a possible danger to the

integrity, availability, or confidentiality

of information systems. An attack,

on the other hand, is a deliberate and

planned action aimed at breaching a

system’s security. Attackers use various

techniques to bypass security measures,

causing damage, stealing data, or

disrupting services. Threats often exploit

vulnerabilities in systems, human errors,

and technological weaknesses to gain

unauthorized access or disrupt normal

operations. Cyberattacks specifically

target computer data and resources

to

compromise

system

security.

Understanding the different types of

cyber threats and attacks is crucial for

developing

effective

cybersecurity

strategies

that

protect

sensitive

information and ensure system resilience.

Types of cyber threats

Cyber threats come in many forms, targeting individuals, organisations,

and governments. There are two basic

categories of cyber threats, natural and

manmade threats.

Natural threats
