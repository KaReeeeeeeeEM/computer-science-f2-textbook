Activity 2.3:

Understanding measures for protection

from natural threats

Aim: To understand key measures for

protecting computer systems and data

from natural threats such as floods,

fires, and power outages. Learners will

identify practical ways to safeguard their

devices and data against environmental

hazards.

Materials: Access to a computer or mobile device with Internet

connectivity, pen and paper or a

computer for note-taking, links or

handouts explaining disaster recovery

strategies and protective technologies,

examples or illustrations of protective

setups (provided by the teacher)

Instructions:

1. Learn about protection measures:

(a) Read the handout or resource

provided on natural threats and

protection measures.

(b) Take notes on the key protection

strategies. Focus on:

(i) Why backing up data is

important and how to do it

securely.

(ii) Where computers should be

placed to avoid environmental

damage.

(iii) How electrical devices

like UPS protect computer

systems.

(iv) What tools or environments help protect computers from fire and heat.

(v) Why it is necessary to maintain proper temperature and humidity levels.

2. Case study research:

(a) Use your research skills to find at least two examples of how organizations

or

individuals

protected their IT systems from

natural threats (such as, during a

flood, fire, or power outage).

(b) Write a short paragraph about each

case,

explaining

the

protection methods used and their effectiveness.

3. Create a natural threat protection plan:

(a) Based on what you learned, write

down five key measures you can

apply at home or school to protect a computer from natural threats.

(b) These

could

include

backup

methods, choosing a safe room for

computer use, and using electrical

or fire protection devices.

Deliverable: A written summary of

key protection measures from natural

threats; Two real-life case studies on

data or system protection; A list of

five protective practices to implement.

Compile these into a portfolio.
