Two-factor authentication

With 2FA, you need to provide a second

piece of information, like a code sent to

your phone. This makes your account

even safer.

Google two-step verification

(i) Sign in to your Google Account

on a web browser or through a

Google app.

(ii) Go to “myaccount.google.com”

to manage your account.

(iii) In the navigation panel, select

“Security”.

(iv) Alternatively, you can click on

the “Security” tab on the “My

Account” page.

(v) Under “How you sign in to

Google,” find and select “2-Step

Verification”.

(vi) Click “Get started”.

Note: Google will send a notification to your phone when you sign in, and

you’ll need to approve it. You’ll receive

a verification code via text message or

voice call. Use an authenticator app

(like Google Authenticator or Authy) to generate verification codes. Use a

physical security key that plugs into your

computer or uses Bluetooth.

Or

(i) Open your Google Account.

(ii) In the navigation panel, select

Security.

(iii) Under “How you sign in to Google,”

select Turn on 2-Step Verification.

(iv) Follow the on-screen steps.

Activity 2.7:

Creating the 2-step verification

Use online resources, such as official

guides or video tutorials, to enable

two-step verification on your Google

account. Follow the steps to link a secondary method, such as a phone

number or authentication app, for added

security. Once set up, test the process to

ensure your account is protected from unauthorised access.
