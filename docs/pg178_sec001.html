Operators and expressions

An operator is a special symbol used to instruct the compiler to carry out specific

mathematical or logical operations. The C language includes many built-in operators,

which are categorised into the following types:

(a) Arithmetic operators

(b) Relational operators

(c) Logical operators

(d) Bitwise operators

(e) Assignment operators

(f) Miscellaneous operators
