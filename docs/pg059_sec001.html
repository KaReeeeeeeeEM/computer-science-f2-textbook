Importance of studying cybersecurity

Studying cybersecurity is important because it helps protect data and safeguard

system resources from cybercriminals. As more people use the Internet for things like

shopping, banking, and working, the risks of cyber threats have grown. By learning

about cybersecurity, people can keep data safe, prevent identity theft, and avoid losing

money. It also teaches us how to deal with cyberattacks and protect our devices and

systems from damage. Knowing about cybersecurity is key to staying safe and keeping

our online information private as technology keeps growing.

Components of cybersecurity

The components of cybersecurity are the key elements that work together to protect information, computer systems, and networks from cyber threats. The components of cybersecurity are summarised in Table 2.1.

Table 2.1: Components of cybersecurity

Component

Description

Confidentiality

Ensure that sensitive data is only accessed or viewed by authorised

individuals, systems or processes.

Integrity

Make sure that the data is accurate and hasn’t been tampered with.

Availability

Make sure that information and systems are available when

needed and not disrupted by attacks.

Authentication

Verify the identity of users, devices, or systems to prevent

unauthorised access.

Access control

Restrict access to systems and data based on users’ roles and

permissions.

Network security

Protect networks from intrusions and attacks, ensuring safe data transmission.

Application security

Ensure that software and applications are free from vulnerabilities

and are secure.

Incident response

Develop a plan to respond to and recover from cybersecurity

incidents like breaches or attacks.
