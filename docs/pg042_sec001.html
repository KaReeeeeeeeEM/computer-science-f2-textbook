Exercise 1.4

1. Aisha is a Form Two student who has

been asked by her teacher to submit

her assignment via email. She has

typed her work using a computer and

saved it as a Word document. Now,

she needs to send it to her teacher’s

email address.

Task:

(i) List the steps Aisha should

follow to compose and send

her email containing the

attachment.

(ii) Explain why it is important to

use a clear subject line and a

polite message in the email.

2. John receives an email from an

unknown sender claiming that he has

won a big prize and needs to click a link to claim it. The email asks for his personal information, including his password.

Tasks:

(i) Identify two signs that show this email could be a scam.

(ii) What steps should John take

to protect himself from email

scams and phishing attacks?

3. Compose a new email and send it by

filling in the “To,” “CC,” and “BCC”

fields correctly. Send the main email

to your fellow student, copy another

friend’s email address in the “CC”

field, and add your second email

address (or an address provided by

your teacher) in the “BCC” field.

After sending, explain to your

teacher the difference between how

CC and BCC were used.

4. How can email communication

be used effectively to enhance professional

relationships

and

workplace productivity in today’s

digital environment?
