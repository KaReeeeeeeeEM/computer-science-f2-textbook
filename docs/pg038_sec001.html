Composing and sending an email

On the email account home page, you

can start composing and sending emails

by clicking on compose. A web page

is displayed for you to fill in the email

address of the receiver, the subject or message heading, and the message to

be sent. Fill in the required information and click the send button. The message will be sent to a person bearing the typed email address. More descriptions of the

fields given in the email composition

window are presented next.

To field

To field is a place to insert the email address of the receiver of a message.

If the message is intended for multiple

receivers, then all receivers’ email

addresses are typed in the “To” field,

separated by a comma.

CC field

CC field is a place to insert the email

address of a receiver to whom you want

to send a copy of the email you have sent

to another receiver. CC is a short form of

Carbon Copy. For instance, your class

teacher can email to congratulate you on

your outstanding academic performance.

Step 3: Access and view your inbox

(a) After signing in, your email dashboard will appear.

(b) Click on the Inbox folder to view your messages.

(c) Open the most recent email-

usually a welcome message from your email provider

(such as, Yahoo’s “Welcome

to email that gets you”).

Deliverables: Successful login to your

email account, A screenshot or note confirming you viewed at least one

message in your inbox.

NOTE: In addition to the Inbox, other

folders displayed on the left-hand side

of your web browser are unread, starred,

sent, spam, archive, and trash. The

descriptions of some of these folders are given next.

(a) Sent: A folder containing messages

that have been sent from your

account.

(b) Draft: A folder containing messages that are written but not sent.

(c) Spam: A folder containing messages received from suspicious sources.

(d) Trash: Folder where deleted emails are temporaly stored.
