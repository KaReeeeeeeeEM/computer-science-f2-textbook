Activity 1.2:

Exploring the historical background

of the Internet

Aim: To introduce the history and

development of the Internet, including key milestones and figures involved in its creation.

Materials: Exercise books or A4

papers, smartphone, tablet or computer

with Internet access; Educational video or reading materials based on

the following search phrase: “History

of the Internet – animated explanation”

(or video pre-downloaded by teacher).

Instructions:

1. Use a search engine to find a short

educational video or article that

explains the history of the Internet.

Look for content that includes

animations or illustrations to help

you visualize key events. If Internet

access is not available, the teacher

may provide pre-downloaded or

printed materials.

2. Watch videos or read the materials

explaining the history of the Internet

carefully and take notes on the most

important points.

Task:

(i) Identify crucial events in the

history of the Internet. Explain

why do you think they are vital.

(ii) Identify two key people who

contributed to the development

of the Internet and describe their main ideas. Then, explain which

idea you find most interesting and

why.

(iii)Create a simple timeline on paper

showing major milestones (with

short descriptions).

(iv)Answer this question in your own

words: Why is it essential for

students to understand the history of the Internet?”
