BCC field

BCC is also a place to insert the email

addresses of receivers other than the

intended ones. BCC is the short form of

Blind Carbon Copy. It works in a similar

way to the “CC” field. Its difference with

CC is that the main receiver of the email

in the “To” field becomes unaware of the

receivers in the “BCC” field. The “BCC”

field is, therefore, used to confidentially

send a copy of an email to other receivers.

Activity 1.15:

Composing an email with an attachment

Use a computer, tablet, or smartphone to sign in to your email account and compose an email with an attachment by following the steps below:

(i) Click 'Compose' to create a new email.

(ii) Type the recipient’s email address.

(iii) Write a 'clear subject' such as

“Assignment Submission”.

(iv) Attach a file by clicking the

'paperclip icon' and selecting a

document.

(v) Write a short message explaining the attachment.

(vi) Click 'Send'.

Sign out or Log out

Sign-out is the process of quitting

(logging out of) a signed-in email account. It is important to sign out to prevent unauthorised users from

accessing information in your email when

someone else uses the computer you have

used to sign in. To sign out of your email account, you need to click a link labelled

Sign Out.

Reading an email message

Reading emails and their attachments is an important skill for effective communication. To read an email

message, open the inbox folder, then click

the subject of the message you want to

read. For example, to read a new message

from Yahoo, click on its subject to open

it. If the message contains an attachment,

you can download it and either open or save it.
