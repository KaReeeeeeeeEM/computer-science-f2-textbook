Activity 1.3:

Exploring the advantages and

disadvantages of using the Internet

Aim: To understand the positive and

negative effects of using the Internet.

Materials: Exercise books or writing

paper, smartphones, tablet or computers

with Internet access, Educational video

or article related to the search phrase:

“Advantages and disadvantages of the

Internet” (or video pre-downloaded by

teacher).

Instructions:

1. Use a search engine to find

educational content (video or

article) that explains the advantages

and disadvantages of the Internet.

You may also use pre-downloaded

materials provided by the teacher.

2. Watch or read the content carefully, noting down the main points.

Task:

(i) List at least three advantages of using the Internet.

(ii) List at least three disadvantages

of using the Internet.

(iii)Choose one advantage and one

disadvantage, and explain briefly

in 2–3 sentences.

(iv)Create a mini-poster or chart

showing the pros and cons of the

Internet, with simple drawings or

symbols if possible.

Deliverables: The findings in a one-

page summary with responses to the

given task.
