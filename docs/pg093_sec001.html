Antivirus

Antivirus software protects your device from harmful programs, such as viruses, malware, and spyware. These programs

can damage your computer or steal

personal information. Without antivirus, your device can get infected, which may

slow it down or cause the loss of important

files. Antivirus software scans for threats

and blocks them. It stops harm before it

happens. It also updates regularly to fight

new types of attacks. Using an antivirus is an important way to keep your devices and data safe.

Reporting cybercrime

Reporting cybercrime is an essential step

in stopping online criminals and protecting

yourself and others. Cybercrimes, such

as hacking, identity theft, cyberbullying,

and online fraud, should be reported to

the police or cybersecurity agencies as

soon as possible. Many countries have

special cybercrime units where victims

can file complaints. It is also helpful to

report cybercrimes to websites, social media platforms, or banks if financial fraud is involved. Keeping evidence,

such as emails, messages, or screenshots,

can help in investigations. Reporting cybercrime helps law enforcement

catch criminals and prevent others from becoming victims.
