Online marketing

In e-marketing, data about customer

behaviours, preferences, needs, and buying patterns are collected. This is done through

e-commerce software and websites. These

tools help in marketing and purchasing

activities such as price fixing, negotiation,

product features enhancement, customer

relationship, purchase, and customer support. E-commerce also includes

advertising products through other sites

and software such as Google ads or ads

in YouTube videos.

Activity 1.22:

Using the Internet for Business

Aim: To help students understand how online shopping works and how it can be used for business purposes.

Materials: A computer, tablet, or smartphone; Internet access; Access

to an e-commerce site (such as, eBay,

Amazon, Jumia, Kikuu or a local

platform)

Instructions:

1. Open an e-commerce website such

as eBay, Amazon, Kikuu or any

reliable online shopping platform.

2. Search for a school-related item

such as: A textbook, School uniform,

Adapter,

Scientific

calculator, Tablet or a laptop

3. Record the following information:

(a) Item name and price

(b) Seller’s name or company

(c) Available delivery or shipping options

4. Summarise your findings by answering:

(a) Was the shopping process easy or difficult?

(b) What are the advantages of online shopping?

(c) What are some possible risks or concerns?

Deliverables:

1. A short written summary (5-7

sentences) of your online shopping

experience

2. A table with details of at least one item, including:

(a) Item Name

(b) Price

(c) Seller

(d) Delivery Options

Document your findings in a portfolio
