The Internet services and applications

The

Internet

provides

several

communication services such as email,

e-learning, e-commerce, newsgroups,

online forums, video and teleconferencing,

and social media.

Electronic mail

Electronic mail (email) is a system for

sending and receiving digital messages

between people using electronic devices

such as computers, tablets, or mobile phones

over

computer

networks,

especially the Internet. Communication

through email requires a person to

register (sign up) on an electronic mail

offered freely or through charged services

by a particular organization. In an email

account, a person can create, save, and

receive information electronically. An

example of an email address is director. general@tie.go.tz.
