General security measures for protection from natural cyber threats

Natural threats can cause considerable damage to your computer. Table 2.4 explains

the measures that you can take to protect your data and computer from natural threats.

Table 2.4: Measures to protect against natural cyber threats

Measure

Description

Back up data

Data backup involves making multiple copies of your data. Since natural disasters like floods and earthquakes can happen unexpectedly, having

backups ensures you can recover your data if it is lost.

For better protection, keep a copy of important data in a separate location, such as a different building, city, or in cloud.

Install computers in secure locations

Position your computer in a location that is unlikely

to be susceptible to harm from natural elements.

To illustrate, it is advisable to refrain from putting computers in rooms that are subject to an excessive amount of dust or moisture.
