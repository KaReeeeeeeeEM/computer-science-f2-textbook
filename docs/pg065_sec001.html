Activity 2.2:

Understanding cybersecurity threats and how to protect yourself

Aim: To understand different types of

cyberattacks such as phishing, denial-

of-service (DoS), identity-based attacks,

and others. To learn how to identify

these threats and protect yourself online.

Materials: Access to a computer or

mobile device with Internet connectivity,

access to YouTube for video viewing,

pen and paper or a computer for note- taking, links to articles or resources on cybersecurity threats, a list of security

practices to follow (provided by the

teacher)

Instructions:

1. Phishing and cybersecurity threats:

(a) Watch the video on YouTube

titled “Understanding phishing

and other cybersecurity threats”

(or use the video pre-downloaded

by the teacher).

(b) Take notes on the key points discussed in the video. Focus on:

(i) How do phishing attacks work, and what should you look out for?

(ii) Different types of cyberattacks (such as

DoS, identity theft, data breaches, etc.).

(iii) Common signs of phishing

emails and how to avoid falling

for scams.

(iv) How to protect personal information online.

2. Research on cybersecurity threats:

(a) After watching the video, use your

research skills to find at least two

examples of real-life phishing or

DoS attacks from the Internet.

(b) Write a short paragraph about each

example, explaining how the attack

happened and its impact.

3. Create a cybersecurity plan:

(a) Based on what you learnt, write down five tips for protecting your personal information online.

(b) These tips could include creating

strong

passwords,

avoiding

suspicious links, and using two-

factor authentication.

Deliverable: A written summary of the

video and its key points;

A list of personal cybersecurity practices

that you can adopt to protect yourself

online.

Document your findings in a portfolio.
