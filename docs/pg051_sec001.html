Student’s Book Form Two

Activity 1.23:

Ethical use of the Internet

Aim: To educate on the ethical use of the Internet by exploring digital

citizenship, copyright issues, and the

impact of cyberbullying.

Materials: Exercise books or writing

paper, tablet, smartphones, or computer

with Internet access (if available); pen,

and paper for note-taking; printed

handouts or online resources on Internet

ethics (optional); YouTube video on:

“Ethical use of the Internet” (or video pre-downloaded by teacher).

Instructions: Search for and watch a

YouTube video titled “Ethical use of

the Internet” or something similar that explains the importance of responsible

Internet use, focusing on topics such

as digital citizenship, respecting others online, and the ethical use of content.

Tasks:

Task 1: Digital citizenship and ethical responsibilities

(i) Research what it means to

be a digital citizen and the

responsibilities that come with

using the Internet.

(ii) Write down key points on how

online users can show respect,

engage in positive behaviours,

and ensure their actions do not

harm others or violate their

rights.

Task 2: Copyright

violations

and

ethical use of intellectual

property

(i) Investigate real-world cases

where people or organisations

have violated copyright laws, such as using someone else’s

work (such as, music, images,

or videos) without permission.

(ii) Research the consequences of

copyright violations and why it’s

important to respect intellectual property online.

Task 3: Cyberbullying and its consequences

Research what “cyberbullying” is and

the potential consequences it can have on both the victims and perpetrators.

Deliverable: A written reflection (1 to 2

pages) on the ethical use of the Internet,

covering digital citizenship, copyright,

and cyberbullying. In reflection,

address:

(i) Why digital citizenship is important and how students can

demonstrate responsible online

behavior.

(ii) The significance of respecting

intellectual property and avoiding

copyright violations.

(iii) The impact of cyberbullying and

steps you can take to help prevent

it in your community.

Document your findings in a portfolio
