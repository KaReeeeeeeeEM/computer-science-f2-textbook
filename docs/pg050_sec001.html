Ethical and responsible use of the Internet

The Internet is an essential tool for communication, education, business,

entertainment, and socialization. However, with its vast potential comes the

responsibility to use it wisely and ethically. Engaging with the digital world ethically

means more than just following laws. It also involves creating an environment of

respect, fairness, and responsibility. When interacting with others online, it is

important to consider how our actions affect them. We must ensure that our digital

footprint is positive and constructive. Figure 1.20 summarises some key ethical

principles for using the Internet.
