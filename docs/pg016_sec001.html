Activity 1.4:

Exploring websites and home pages

Aim: To help students understand the

structure and purpose of a website,

identify the features of a home page,

and observe how hyperlinks connect

different web pages.

Materials:A device with Internet access

(computer, tablet, or smartphone); OR

a printed screenshot of a website home

page (For example, www.tie.go.tz, www.

necta.go.tz); Pen or pencil; Exercise

book or printed activity worksheet.

Instructions:

Step 1: Observe the website.

Open one of the following

websites or view the screenshot

provided:

(a) https://www.tie.go.tz

(b) https://www.necta.go.tz

(NECTA)

Look carefully at the home page (the

first page that opens when you enter the

website.)

Step 2: Explore and record.

In your exercise book or worksheet,

answer

these

questions:

(a) Website name:

_________________________

(b) Purpose of the website:

_________________________

(c) Main heading or title on the home page:

_________________________

(d) Three things you can see on the home page:

(e) One hyperlink found on the page:

(i) Text on the link:

____________________

(ii) Where it leads:

____________________

(f) How can you tell this is the home page?

Step 3: Reflect

Complete

this

sentence:

“A home page is important

because...”

Deliverables: Write a summary of

responses as per instruction given.
