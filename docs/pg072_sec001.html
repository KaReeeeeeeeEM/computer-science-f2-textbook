Grey hat hackers

They fall somewhere in between, sometimes hacking without permission but with no malicious intent, and often report vulnerabilities to the affected parties after discovering them.

Ways hackers exploit vulnerabilities

Hackers are people who use computer skills to access systems or data without

permission. Some hackers help improve security (ethical hackers), while others

steal information or cause damage (black hat hackers). They often exploit weak

passwords, outdated software, or trick people through phishing emails. Hackers may

also use malicious code, attack public Wi-Fi, or spread viruses and ransomware. To

stay safe, use strong passwords, keep your software updated, and avoid clicking on

suspicious links.

Case studies of major cyber attacks

When using a mobile phone, you may receive text messages from unknown people,

some with links or requests for money. These messages are often scams designed to

steal your money or personal information.

These cyberattacks, such as phishing, malware, and identity theft, are common

worldwide, including in Tanzania. They mainly target mobile money users and

bank accounts with weak security. Always be cautious with unknown messages and

avoid clicking suspicious links. Case studies 2.1-2.6 outline some major worldwide

cyberattacks that highlight different types of cyber threats and their impacts.

Case study 2.1: WannaCry ransomware Attack (2017)

(i) Attack type: Ransomware

(ii) Target: Computers running Microsoft Windows

(iii) Impact: Affected over 200,000 computers in 150 countries, including hospitals, banks, and businesses.

(iv) Cause: Exploited a Windows vulnerability known as EternalBlue (leaked

NSA exploit).

(v) Lesson: Keeping software updated and using backups can prevent such attacks
