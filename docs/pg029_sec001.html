Activity 1.10:

Modem configuration and settings

Aim: Understand how to access,

configure, and secure a Wi-Fi modem

by exploring basic modem settings and connection information.

Materials: A Wi-Fi-enabled modem

or router; A computer or smartphone;

Ethernet cable (optional); Internet

Service Provider (ISP) login credentials

(username and password); Access to the

modem’s IP address (usually provided on the modem label or in the manual)

Instructions: Use a Wi-Fi-enabled modem along with a computer or

smartphone to access and configure the modem settings by following the steps below:

Step 1: Locate the modem’s default

username

and

password,

usually provided by your ISP

or printed on the modem label.

Step 2: Connect your device to the

modem via Wi-Fi or Ethernet cable.

Step 3: Open a web browser and

enter the modem’s IP address

in the address bar (such as,

192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1).

Step 4: Enter the username and

password when prompted to

log into the modem’s settings dashboard.

Step 5: Explore the modem interface

and adjust basic settings,

such as:

(a) Renaming the Wi-Fi network

(SSID)

(b) Changing the Wi-Fi password

(c) Enabling the strongest available

Wi-Fi encryption (such as

WPA3).

(d) Managing Ethernet (wired) settings

Step 6: Find the connection status or network information section and note:

(a) Your IP address

(b) WAN/DSL connection status

(c) Connection

speed

and

bandwidth usage

Step 7: Click logout to safely exit the

modem interface.

Step 8: Test the Internet connection

by connecting to the updated

Wi-Fi network or browsing

a webpage.

Deliverables: Screenshot or notes

showing access to the modem settings

page; List of changes made (such as, SSID name, password update);

Recorded connection information, such

as IP address and status; Confirmation that the Internet connection works after configuration.
