Safe browsing for social media

Social media can be useful, fun and

engaging, but it also comes with risks,

such as cyber threats, scams, and privacy

concerns. The following are some safe

browsing tips to protect yourself while

using social media.

(i)

Create complex passwords with

a mix of uppercase, lowercase,

numbers, and symbols.

(ii) Adds an extra layer of security to prevent unauthorised access, i.e.,

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

(2FA).

(iii) Use a different password for your

email, as it can be used to reset

social media passwords.

(iv) Do not unnecessarily post personal

details like your address, phone

number, or financial information

or if you are unsure of whom it

may be.

(v)

Avoid posting real-time locations to prevent tracking.

(vi) Once something is online, it can be hard to remove completely.

(vii) Regularly check and update your

privacy settings to control who can

see your posts. Keep your profile

private where possible to limit exposure to strangers. Revoke

access to apps you no longer use.

(viii) Do not click on suspicious links

sent via messages or emails. Do

not accept requests from unknown

or suspicious accounts. Scammers

may impersonate people you know.

(ix) Avoid sharing sensitive information such as passwords or OTPs, even with close friends.

(x)

Report suspicious activity such

as fake accounts, harassment, or

scams.

(xi) Be wary of unusual requests, such

as friends asking for money or

help.

(xii) Avoid public Wi-Fi. You may use a

VPN when accessing social media

on public networks.

(xiii) Always log out when using a public or shared computer.

(xiv) Ensure the website URL starts with

“https://” for a secure connection.

(xv) Update software regularly to

help fix security vulnerabilities

by patching known flaws that

attackers could exploit.
