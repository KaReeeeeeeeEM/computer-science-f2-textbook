E-learning platforms and online education

The growth of the Internet has transformed

how people access education, giving rise to e-learning the use of electronic technologies to deliver and receive

educational content outside a traditional

classroom. E-learning platforms are

digital systems or websites that enable

teaching and learning to occur online,

without the need for physical classrooms.

These platforms provide tools for course

delivery, assessments, communication,

and student progress tracking. E-learning

allows learners to access materials through

devices such as computers, tablets, or

smartphones. It includes resources like

recorded lessons, digital notes, interactive

quizzes, and presentations. Whether a

student watches a video lesson at home or completes an online assignment, they are engaging in e-learning.
