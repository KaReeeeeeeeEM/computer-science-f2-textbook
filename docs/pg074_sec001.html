Case study 2.6: Colonial pipeline

ransomware attack

(2021)

(i)

Attack type: Ransomware

(ii) Target: Colonial pipeline (largest fuel pipeline in the U.S.)

(iii) Impact: Fuel shortages across the

U.S. East Coast; the company

paid $4.4 million in ransom.

(iv) Cause: A single compromised

password allowed hackers to

enter the system.

(v) Lesson: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and better cybersecurity training can prevent such incidents.

Cybercrimes

Cybercrime is the use of computers or

digital devices to carry out illegal or harmful activities online. It includes actions like hacking, online scams, cyberbullying,

identity

theft,

and

spreading viruses. Cybercrime can affect

individuals, businesses, or communities

by stealing information, money, or

causing emotional or physical harm.

Classification of cybercrime

Cybercrime is classified based on the target and method used. The main

categories include:

(i)

Against individuals: Involves identity

theft,

phishing,

cyberstalking, and online scams

that target personal data or finances.

(ii) Against organisations: Targets businesses through data breaches,

corporate espionage, ransomware,

and system disruption.
