Historical background of the Internet

The “Internet Evolution Timeline” shows

how the Internet has grown and changed

from 1969 to the 2020s. It started with

ARPANET in 1969, one of the first computer networks, and later in the

1970s, the basic rules for how computers

talk to each other (called TCP/IP) were

created. In the 1980s, people began using

email and the Domain Name System

(DNS), which made it easier to find

websites. The 1990s brought the World

Wide Web, making the Internet useful for

everyone, and many companies started

building websites, leading to the dot-com

boom. In the 2000s and beyond, faster

Internet (broadband), smartphones, social

media, cloud storage, video streaming,

5G, and smart devices (IoT) have made

the Internet a big part of everyday life.
