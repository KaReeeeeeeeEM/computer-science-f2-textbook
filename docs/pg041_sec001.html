Activity 1.18:

Identifying and opening attachments

Follow these steps to identify and open attachments:

(i) Look for an attachment icon

(paperclip symbol

) in the

email.

(ii) Identify the file type such as .pdf,

.doc, .ppt, .jpg.

(iii) Click on the attachment to preview it.

(iv) Click Download to save the file.

(v) Click on Save: a page that shows

different locations where the file

can be saved will be opened.

(vi) Select a location, such as the

Desktop, then click Save. The

file will be saved on the Desktop.

The attached file can be seen in terms

of its size and file extension. For Yahoo

Mail, the maximum file size to be

attached should not exceed 25 MB. Other

email applications might have different

maximum file sizes. If you want to

attach another file, you may click on the

Attach More button. Complete the task by

clicking Send. The message will be sent

and the feedback report will be displayed.

Advantages of email services

Some of the advantages of email services

are as follows:

(i)

It is very easy to send emails and

manage your contacts.

(ii)

You can send email messages instantly to any person in the

world.

(iii) Though you pay for an Internet

connection, the benefits beat the

cost.

(iv) It is reliable because you are

often assured that the email will

be delivered to the recipient’s

address.

(v)

It is easier to send much longer

messages with email than with

Short Message Service (SMS).

(vi) It allows a user to send different

attachments such as text, a picture,

and a video.

(vii) It is easy to refer to the previous email.

(viii) With the increased availability of the Internet on mobile phones,

email is becoming accessible to

most parts of the country and the world.

(ix) Using email allows you to format

the message based on your

preferences.

Disadvantages of email services

The following are some of the

disadvantages of using email services:

(i)

Some emotions or personal feelings are difficult to express

clearly through typed messages.

(ii)

Users must have basic computer

skills to use email effectively.
