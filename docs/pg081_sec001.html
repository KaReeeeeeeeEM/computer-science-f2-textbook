Activity 2.5:

Authentication and password security - creating a password

Aim: To learn how to create strong passwords, protect accounts, and understand the potential risks of weak passwords.

Materials: Computer or smartphone with Internet access, access to YouTube,

paper and pen for note-taking

Instructions: Watch a short YouTube video explaining how to create a strong

password based on the provided guidelines

Task:

1. Watch a YouTube video about password authentication, password security

guidelines, things to avoid, weak passwords, and how to choose a strong

password. The video should cover the following topics:

(i) Password authentication: What is it, and why is it important?

(ii) Guideline for setting secure passwords: Best practices for creating strong passwords.

(iii) Changing your password: The importance of regularly updating your password.

(iv) Things to avoid: What habits make passwords weak?
