5. What are the leading causes of

Ethical issues and related

principles

Ethical issues involve deciding what is

right or wrong when values conflict. Core

principles like honesty, fairness, respect,

and responsibility guide these decisions.

For example, returning a lost wallet

reflects these values. In the digital world,

the same principles apply where users

must act ethically when using cyberspace

to support a fair and respectful society.

Data privacy

Data privacy involves protecting personal

information shared online or stored on

digital devices. This includes names,

addresses, and contact details, which

can be misused if not secured. To protect

data:

(i) Be mindful of what you share

online.

(ii) Use strong passwords and privacy

settings.

(iii) Avoid suspicious websites.

(iv) Read privacy policies and only

provide necessary information.

Individuals and organisations are responsible for protecting data, and laws exist to ensure proper handling,

Forexample National Personal Data

Protection Act 2022 of Tanzania. Staying

informed helps keep personal information

safe.

Cybersecurity ethics

Cybersecurity ethics are moral principles

that guide the safe and responsible use

of technology and the Internet. This

includes protecting personal information,

respecting others’ privacy, and avoiding harmful actions like hacking, spreading

viruses, or stealing data. Key practices

include:

(i) Respecting others online.

(ii) Avoiding cyberbullying and false information.

(iii) Not accessing or using others’ data without permission.

(iv) Avoiding illegal activities like piracy or fraud.

(v) Giving credit to original creators.

While laws help protect users, individuals

must also act ethically to create a safer

and more respectful digital environment.

Being a responsible digital citizen benefit

everyone.
