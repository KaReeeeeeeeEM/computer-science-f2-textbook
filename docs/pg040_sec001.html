Activity 1.16:

Accessing and reading an email

Use a computer, tablet, or smartphone to access and read emails by following these steps:

(i) Open your email account (Gmail,

Yahoo, or Outlook).

(ii) Go to your Inbox and find an unread email.

(iii) Click on the email to open and

read its content.

(iv) Identify the following in the

email:

(a) Sender’s name

(b) Subject line

(c) Main message

(v) Write down the purpose of the

email in one sentence.

NOTE: After reading the email message,

you can delete the message, print it, reply

to it, or forward it to other recipients. For

example, to forward the message, click on

the Forward button that appears above the

message, and the Compose Message page

will appear for you to fill in the details as per the procedure for sending an email.

Activity 1.17:

Replying to an email

Use a computer, tablet, or smartphone

to reply to emails by following these steps:

(i) Choose an email that requires a response.

(ii) Click Reply or Reply All.

(iii) Write a short, clear response.

For example, “Thank you for

your email. I have received the

attachment and will review it

soon”.

(iv) Check for spelling and grammar before sending.

(v) Click Send.

NOTE: The computer allows you to

choose the location where you can find the file you want to attach. In this case,

the options are Desktop, Downloads,

Recent places, or other locations on

your computer. When you click on the

Desktop, all folders and files stored in

the desktop folder will appear. Select the file names, then click the Open button to attach the file.
