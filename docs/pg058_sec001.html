Meaning of cybersecurity

The foundation of cybersecurity centres

on the term cyber. The term “cyber” relates

to computer systems, digital technology,

and the Internet or online world. It is

often used to describe things connected

to online activities, networks, and virtual

environments, such as cyberspace,

cybercrime, and cybersecurity.

Cyberspace is the virtual world created

by computers, the Internet, and digital

networks. As discussed in the previous

chapter, the world of the Internet is

what makes the cyberspace of our days.

It is where online activities, such as communication, social media, online banking, and gaming, take place. In cyberspace, people can connect, share

information, and do business. However, it also comes with risks like cybercrime.

Cybercrime is a crime committed using

computers, the Internet, or other digital

devices. It includes hacking, stealing

personal information, spreading harmful

viruses, online scams, cyberbullying, and

identity theft. Criminals use technology

to steal money and personal data or harm

people and businesses. To stay safe, it is

important to protect personal information

and follow good cybersecurity practices.

Cybersecurity is the practice of keeping

computer systems, networks, and data

safe from hackers or other unauthorised

access. It includes using technology,

rules, and safety measures to prevent

cyber-attacks. The goal of cybersecurity

is to protect computer systems, networks,

applications, and data from unauthorized

access, damage, or disruption. It aims

to ensure the confidentiality, integrity,

and availability of information and

digital resources, so they remain secure,

accurate, and accessible to authorized

users when needed.
