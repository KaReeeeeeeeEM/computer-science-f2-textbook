7. Program maintenance

Program maintenance involves updating and modifying software after release to

fix errors, enhance performance, remove outdated parts, and add new features. Its goal is to ensure the system continues to meet user needs and functions as intended.

Maintenance types include corrective, preventive, perfective, and adaptive.
