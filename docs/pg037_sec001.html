Signing in and accessing email folders

In general, the term “sign in” means recording arrival at a place such as a

hotel, office, or workstation by writing

in a special guest book. In emails, signing

in means accessing your email account

to send new messages or read incoming messages.

Accessing an email

email account and view messages in the

inbox.

Use a computer, tablet, or smartphone to sign in to your already-created email

mail.google.com,
