Install protective electrical devices

Install uninterruptible power supply (UPS) devices to ensure battery backup during power outages. A

UPS safeguards against software damage resulting from sudden computer shutdowns.

Insulate computer from fire

Protect the computers from fire by enclosing them

in fire-resistant environments. Furthermore, you

have the option to implement sufficient fire safety

apparatus and protocols to promptly mitigate any

potential harm.

Maintain appropriate temperature or humidity

To guarantee optimal performance of your computer,

it is essential to maintain the ideal temperature and

humidity levels. This may be achieved by adding

equipment such as air conditioners and humidity

controls.
